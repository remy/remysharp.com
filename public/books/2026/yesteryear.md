Excellent psychological thriller.

I really enjoyed this book from debut author Caro Claire Burke - reading it on holiday over 5 days, probably not the "ideal" holiday read though - it's heavy stuff.

Following the character of Natalie - who from the first few chapters I really quickly disliked their entire ethos. But it's never as simple as a horrible person, and we're able to look under the surface of what made this Tradwife and the impact on her life.

The story is split between two times where Natalie is in the modern world and one that's very much not the modern world (where activities include washing clothes takes the entire day and leave her hands completely numb).

It's hard to talk about the details of the story without giving too much up about the arc of the story - which I don't want to give away.

I will say that it's brilliantly written and the characters are wonderfully complicated and flawed, and the Natalie character is written as complex without being compromising.

The content is hard work, and if like me you're not a fan of the Tradwife movement and what it really represents in society, the payoff is worthwhile.
