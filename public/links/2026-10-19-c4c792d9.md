# AI can operate a screen reader. What does that actually test?

Really like the framing of this article. It uses some very visceral (or easy to understand) examples of how people vs. AI would approach the problem space, and my feeling is that this kind of thinking works well when expanding well beyond the scope of accessibility (though this is an excellent place to start).

The floor plan example is good, but it was this that particularly won me over:

> An AI agent starts with advantages a person doesn’t have. […]
>
> The agent knows the goal before it begins. […]
> 
> A pass tells us the agent found a route, not what finding it was like. AI performs; people experience.
