# HUMAN.md by Briana (Mazzio) May

This is a really good bit of writing (and reading). I'm not a _huge_ fan of the animations, partly because they are constantly jittery, but also because there was some jankiness when being forced to scroll to the next part - BUT it did slow down how I read it and let me sit with the thoughts longer.

I particularly liked:

> Our job is to know the rule cold, and then _break it on purpose_, in public, with a reason.

Hard agree on this particular point. 

I also enjoyed how it didn't try to argue for or against AI but to talk think about our (human) role today and acknowledge that this is a working draft that can, should and will change even after 6 months.
