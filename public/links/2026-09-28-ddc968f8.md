# There are no \"rogue\" AI agents

I've read so many posts in the last few months about AI agents going "rogue" and this post does a good job of calling absolute bullshit on the language being used by media outlets (and the tech companies themselves).

> AI cannot think for itself, nor can it take independent actions. But anthropomorphizing language suggesting it can is how the technology is described.

I keep saying this, but the companies _are_ responsible and should be prosecuted as such. Any other smaller company or individual caught in a cyber attack (which is what these reports actually are) would be likely jailed and banned from using computers again.

Lest we forget Aaron Swartz.
